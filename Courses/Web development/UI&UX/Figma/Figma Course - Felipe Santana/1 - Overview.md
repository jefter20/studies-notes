 #Class1 #Figma
Launched in 2016, Figma came to meet some of Sketch's demands. It's great for collaboration between teams and works in the cloud, allowing teams to collaborate in real time from anywhere in the world.

It is a vector tool, focused on screen design. And because it is a vector tool, it allows you to export the design in different sizes, which is essential nowadays where there are different screen sizes.

And finally, the Handoff tools (delivery), before Figma you needed other programs, buying the license and exporting the entire design, among other things, the process became more expensive and laborious. But with Figma, basically what is shown on the screen is the final project, just share the link with the client, developer, etc. With the link you can extract the color palette, font and also the CSS code.

Figma also runs in the browser.

*Note: In the desktop version, Figma allows you to use color systems on your PC, but Figma only exports in sRGB. So to avoid color differences when exporting your design, configure Figma Desktop to use the sRGB color system.*
