##### **1 -  Configuring the Style guide on Figma**

To begin, define the font, font weight, and size for each text configuration, that is, each text size, in your Style guid, based on Material Design.
![[Pasted image 20250114041737.png]]

Create elements for each text size, and increase the size of the titles with multiples of 4 or 8, as below:

- **H1 =>** Font: your choice, Weight: bold, Size: 56, Line Height: 68
- **H2 =>** Font: your choice, Weight: bold, Size: 48, Line Height: 56
- **H3 =>** Font: your choice, Weight: bold, Size: 40, Line Height: 48
- **H4 =>** Font: your choice, Weight: medium, Size: 32, Line Height: 36
- **H5 =>** Font: your choice, Weight: medium, Size: 24, Line Height: 28
- **H6 =>** Font: your choice, Weight: medium, Size: 20, Line Height: 24
- **Subtitle =>** Font: your choice, Weight: medium, Size: 16, Line Height: 20
- **Body =>** Font: your choice, Weight: regular, Size: 16, Line Height: 20
- **Button =>** Font: your choice, Weight: bold, Size: 14, Line Height: 16
- **Caption =>** Font: your choice, Weight: regular, Size: 12, Line Height: 16
  
Initially set the Line Height of all texts to automatic. Then turn everything into [[Auto Layout]] and set the distance between elements to 24 to have a good space between them.
![[Pasted image 20250114045802.png]]

Now add each element to the style library. Select the element and in the side menu on the right, in {Typography}, button on the right with the four-dot design {Apply Styles}, {+} Create style button, a [[Modal]] will open, and in the {Name} field Enter the name of the folder if you want to group the elements, and its respective name, e.g. Headline/H1 or just H1 to not group the elements, and then click the blue button {Create style}.
![[Pasted image 20250114052140.png]]
![[Pasted image 20250114052816.png]]

To access the style library in Figma, simply click on the canvas (gray frame that occupies almost the entire screen), and then the elements saved in the library will be in the side menu on the right.
![[Pasted image 20250114053852.png]]

And finally, set the Line Hight of the element to a number that is a multiple of 4 to have a quality design on screens of different sizes. Do this for each element, the Line Height can change in elements of other sizes, an H1 for example, can have a Line Height greater than 20, make this adjustment visually to know what suits best.
![[Pasted image 20250114060337.png]]
